Rooted in Oahu

Website Project Site Plan

Site Name

Rooted in Oahu

I chose the name Rooted in Oahu because the website focuses on creating a deeper connection to the island rather than simply presenting Oahu as a tourist destination. The word "rooted" represents connection to the land, culture, traditions, history, and people of Oahu.

Site Purpose

Rooted in Oahu will help visitors learn about and respectfully experience Oahu through Hawaiian culture, traditions, local places, food, history, and connection to the land. The website will encourage visitors to look beyond typical tourist attractions and better understand the culture and community that make Oahu unique.

Scenarios

  • How can I experience Hawaiian culture respectfully while visiting Oahu?
  • Where can I go on Oahu to learn more about Hawaiian history, traditions, and connection to the land?
  • What local foods, activities, and experiences can help me better understand life and culture on Oahu?

Planned Website Content

Home

The home page will introduce Rooted in Oahu and explain the idea of experiencing the island through culture, respect, and connection. It will highlight selected cultural experiences and encourage users to explore the rest of the website.

Culture & Traditions

This page will introduce Hawaiian traditions, values, language, food, history, and cultural practices. It will also provide guidance for visitors on experiencing and learning about Hawaiian culture respectfully.

Explore Oahu

This page will feature meaningful places and experiences around Oahu. Visitors will be able to explore different locations and activities and save experiences they are interested in.

Color Schema

#F3E9D2

Earth Brown

#6B4F3A

Used for secondary headings, borders, and natural accents.

Island Cream

#FFFDF7

Used for cards and areas that need a clean background.

Typography

Playfair Display

Playfair Display will be used for major headings and titles. Its style gives the site a warm, editorial feeling while helping important headings stand out.

DM Sans

DM Sans will be used for paragraphs, navigation, buttons, labels, and other general website content because it is simple and easy to read on both mobile and desktop screens.
